# Privacy Policy – Upvia

**Last updated:** December 28, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Upvia (hereinafter referred to as **“the App”**) respects and protects users’ privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Upvia **does not collect, store, process, or share any type of personal or sensitive user data**, including but not limited to:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location
- Device identifiers
- Usage data
- Biometric information
- Financial information

The App operates entirely **without collecting user data**.

## 3. Device Permissions

Upvia **does not request any device permissions** that grant access to personal or sensitive information.

If future features require additional permissions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Upvia **does not use third-party services** that collect user data, such as:

- Analytics services
- Advertising services
- Tracking tools
- Monitoring services

## 5. Security

Since Upvia does not collect or store user data, no personal information is processed, significantly reducing any risks related to data security.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Upvia can be used by people of all ages and **does not collect personal information from children or adults**.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
